About StreamTV

StreamTV is a student-built streaming platform. It lets visitors explore a library of free films, read more about each title, watch them, and save favourites to their own list.

Who is it for?

Students who want free, legal films to watch.

Film fans who like to browse by genre.

Anyone who wants a simple list of what to watch next.

How it works

  1. Explore the Home page for the featured film and the Trending, New Releases and Popular rows.
  2. Open Browse to search the library or filter it by genre.
  3. Click any film to see its details, then press Watch Now to play it.
  4. Add films to My List. Your list is saved in this browser.
  5. Sign in if you like. It is a demo, so no real account is made.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is StreamTV?

StreamTV is a student project for Principles of Web Design. It is not a real streaming service.

Do I have to sign in?

No. You can browse and watch without signing in. Signing in is a demonstration and only changes the Sign in button to Log out.

Is the sign up real?

No. The sign up and sign in forms are demonstrations. No account is created and no password is stored.

How do I add a title to My List?

Press + My List on any film, on any page, or open the film and press it there.

Why is my list empty on another device?

My List is saved in your browser, so it does not follow you to other browsers or devices.

Contact and Help

Have a question or a problem? Send us a message. This is a demonstration form and the message is not sent anywhere.
